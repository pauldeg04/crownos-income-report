Final Confirmation

Restore CrownOS Backup?

This does not just replace data in this browser. CrownOS syncs to the cloud, so restoring pushes this backup to every other signed-in device and branch — anything entered anywhere since this backup was taken (today's schedules, sales, attendance, everything) will be silently overwritten as soon as those devices next sync, even if someone is using CrownOS on them right now.

A safety backup of the current data will download automatically before restore. That only protects THIS device's pre-restore state, not what any other device may lose.
