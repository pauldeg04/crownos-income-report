Part One

Getting Started

What the system is, how to get into it, and what your account can do.

Chapter 1What CrownOS Is

CrownOS is the official management system of Crown Head Spa. It holds everything that happens day to day: sales, client schedules, staff attendance, stock levels, expenses, and payroll. All branches share one database — a sale recorded in Biñan is visible in Calamba and to management immediately.

Opening the system

CrownOS is a website, so there is nothing to install. Open crownos-5f03d.web.app in your browser (Chrome or Safari) and you will land on the login page.

Tip for branch tablets

You can install CrownOS so it behaves like a real app. In Safari on an iPad, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. In Chrome, look for Install in the address bar. You get a CrownOS icon on the home screen, and the app opens without a browser bar — faster and cleaner for reception.

Four things to know before you start

  • One account per person. Never share your login. Your attendance, commission, and pay are all tied to your account.
  • Branch and Date govern everything. Almost every page follows the Branch and Date set in the toolbar at the top. Get those wrong and you are looking at the wrong data.
  • Saving is automatic. There is no separate "Save All" button. Once you press Save or Settle on a form, it is done and already synced to the cloud.
  • Someone backs up every day. At the end of the day, one person (normally the Admin) exports a backup file. See Chapter 23.

Chapter 2Signing In

  1. Open CrownOS. The Welcome Back login screen appears first.
  2. Enter your Account — the username or email your Admin issued you.
  3. Enter your Password and press Login.
  4. Once inside, choose your Branch from the toolbar at the top.

If you see "Invalid account, password, or inactive user," either one of the two is wrong, or your account has been set to Inactive. Only an Admin can reactivate it, from Account Settings.

"What's Your Duty Today?" — for dual-role staff

Some Therapists occasionally fill in as Receptionist. If Secondary Role: Receptionist is enabled on your account, you are asked which duty you are working right after you log in:

  • Therapist — your regular therapist duty.
  • Receptionist — relieving for today only.
This matters

The duty you pick sets your rate and pay for the whole day, and which pages you can open. The two roles are never combined — if you pick Receptionist, only Receptionist pages are open to you for that day. Choose correctly at the first login of the day.

If you see "Clock Out First," you still have an open clock-in from an earlier session today. Clock out on the Dashboard before switching duty.

Changing your password

Go to Account Settings → My Account → Change Password. Only you can change your own password. If you have forgotten it, an Admin resets it for you.

Signing out

Logout sits at the bottom of the sidebar. Use it rather than just closing the tab. Logging out makes sure everything you did last is pushed to the cloud before you leave.

Shared branch tablets

If a tablet uses a Branch Device account, leave it signed in. That account is deliberately limited to the Dashboard, Client Database, and Scheduling — no sales, no payroll, no reports.

Part Two

Daily Operations

The work of the branch: attendance, sales, clients, schedules, and pay.

Chapter 5Dashboard and Clock In

This is the first page after you log in. It does two jobs: it shows today's branch schedule, and it lets you clock in and out.

Branch Schedule

A timeline laid out by time and bed. The count of Scheduled appointments and the Next Schedule appear at the top. Click any appointment to see its full detail: services to be availed, therapist, bed, date, time, status, notes, and the client's forms.

Timeline colours:

  • Available — the slot is free.
  • Scheduled — booked.
  • Other Therapist — another therapist's booking (as seen by a Therapist account).
  • Companion — a guest sharing the same transaction.
  • Completed — the service is finished.
  • Current Time — the present-time marker.

Clocking in and out

  1. Check the Branch in the toolbar first — that is where your hours will be recorded.
  2. Press Clock In on the Attendance card.
  3. On your first clock-in of the day you are asked to choose Opening (9:00 AM – 6:00 PM) or Closing (1:00 PM – 10:00 PM). Pick the shift you are actually working.
  4. At the end of your shift, return to the Dashboard and press Clock Out.
About arriving early

If you arrive before your shift officially starts, that early time is not counted toward your pay. Counting begins at the official start of the shift you chose. This is exactly why the Opening or Closing choice has to be right — the whole day's computation rests on it.

Location check (GPS)

When you clock in, CrownOS checks whether you are actually at the branch. You need to be within 150 metres of the branch pin for the entry to be marked as location-verified.

  • If you are further away, a warning appears showing the estimated distance.
  • If location is switched off or there is no signal, the entry is marked "No GPS."
  • If the branch has no GPS pin set yet, you are told so — an Admin sets it in List of Branches.
Going-home reminder

Once you have been clocked in for more than 9 hours without clocking out, the system sends a reminder to your notification bell. If you genuinely forget, an Admin corrects it in Attendance.

Chapter 7Recording a Sale

One form covers every kind of transaction: services only, products only, with companions, with vouchers, with a VIP card, with an invoice. Work through it in this order.

1 · Time and client

Time *
Time of the transaction. Set to the current time automatically.
Client *
Start typing and the system suggests matches from the Client Database. For a new client, just type their full name.
Additional Client Details
A panel for the client's full information: Loyalty Card Number, name, Sex, Contact Number, Email, Birthday, Home Address, Home Branch, VIP Status, and Remarks. What you enter here goes straight into the Client Database, so there is no need to repeat it there.
Senior Citizen / PWD ID *
Required when Senior/PWD pricing is used. Enter the ID number.
Source *
Where the client came from: Facebook, Walk-in, Referral, or Returning. This becomes Client Acquisition Sources in Statistics — answer it honestly.
Therapist
Who performed the service. This drives commission and Therapist Sales. Optional only when the transaction is products alone.

2 · Services and products

Six buttons add lines to the transaction:

Add Service
A service from the List of Services. Price and duration are filled in automatically.
Add Freebie
A complimentary service. Its value is still recorded for reporting, but ₱0.00 is charged.
Add Product
A retail product. Enter the quantity — the price multiplies automatically.
Add Consumable
A product used on the client but not sold to them — picked from products marked Available for Consumables in the List of Products. It is never charged (always ₱0.00), but it still deducts from the branch's stock and logs in the Stock Audit exactly like a sold product.
Add VIP Card
Sells a VIP membership card and immediately switches every service in the transaction to VIP pricing.
Add Purchase Voucher
Adds a voucher the client is buying now to this transaction. See Chapter 8.

3 · Automatic pricing

Each service carries four prices in the master list. CrownOS picks which one to use:

Price typeWhen it is used
RegularThe standard price. This is the default.
First TimerThe client's first visit.
VIPApplied automatically when (a) a VIP Card is in the transaction, (b) the client is already VIP in the database, or (c) VIP is the selected price type.
Senior / PWDDiscounted price. Requires an ID number to be entered.
When you need to change a price

You can override an item's amount by hand. Once you do, the system stops recalculating that line even if the price type changes — so check it again before you settle.

4 · Companions

When a client brings someone who also takes a service, do not create a separate sale. Press Add Companion. Each companion gets their own list of services and their own therapist, but the group pays once. This keeps the client count correct in the KPI and gives each therapist the right commission.

5 · Payment and vouchers

Payment can be split. Press Add Payment Method to add a second one — for example ₱1,000 Cash and ₱500 GCash. The payments must add up to the Amount to Pay.

Beside it is the Voucher section, where a voucher being used as payment is entered. Executive Voucher is for complimentary or management-issued vouchers.

Three lines are always visible:

Gross Total the full amount before vouchers Voucher Deduction − the amount the voucher covers Amount to Pay what the client actually pays

6 · Invoice

Tick Issue Invoice when the client asks for a receipt. This reveals Invoice Number (required) and TIN Number (optional). Every invoiced transaction appears in the Sales Invoice Summary.

7 · Finishing — the four buttons

ButtonWhat it doesWhen to use it
Cancel Closes the form without saving. You opened it by mistake.
Add to Schedule Turns it into an appointment in Scheduling, finding a free bed and time automatically. An advance booking or a return visit.
Add to List Places it in Ongoing Transactions — recorded but not yet paid. The client is still in the spa and has not paid.
Settle Completes the transaction. It now counts toward the day's income. The client has paid.

When a client in Ongoing Transactions pays, find their row and press Settle there. It moves into the sales table.

Who can edit

Whether you can edit a settled sale depends on your role. If Edit is missing from a row, that table is closed to your role — pass the correction to an Admin or Executive Assistant.

Saving a sale can move stock

If a service on the sale has inventory items linked to it, saving also deducts those items from the branch's stock and logs them in the branch Stock Audit. Correcting the sale corrects that too, so fix mistakes by editing the sale rather than adjusting stock by hand. See Warehouse and Branches.

Chapter 8Vouchers and VIP Cards

The voucher life cycle

  1. Purchase. In Add Sale, press Add Purchase Voucher. Choose the Voucher Item and enter the buyer's name (required), then press Add to Sale.
  2. Number reserved. The system creates a unique code in the form CHS-XXXX-XXXX and attaches it to this sale as a line item — but it is not yet official and does not appear in the Voucher Masterlist yet.
  3. Settle. Only when this sale is Settled does the voucher become official: it is recorded in the Voucher Masterlist with that same reserved code, and its branded PDF is generated and downloaded automatically — one combined PDF file even if the sale has several vouchers. Saving to Add to List or Add to Schedule keeps it pending; nothing is recorded until it is actually settled.
  4. Redemption. When the holder returns, enter the code in the Voucher section of a new sale. It is deducted from the Amount to Pay.
  5. Closed. After settling that redeeming sale, the code is marked redeemed and cannot be used again.

Every voucher is valid for 6 months from the date it became official (the Settle date, not the draft date). Once that date passes, an unredeemed voucher is automatically treated as Expired — no manual step needed — and can no longer be used as payment.

Voucher messages

MessageWhat it meansWhat to do
Please enter the voucher number A voucher was selected but no code was entered. Type the code from the client's copy.
Voucher … was not found No such code exists in the system. Check the spelling. Search the Voucher Masterlist by the client's name.
Voucher … has been voided This voucher was cancelled. It can no longer be used. Refer to management.
Voucher … has already been used It was redeemed in an earlier transaction. Check Redemption in the Voucher Masterlist to see when it was used.
Voucher … expired on … and can no longer be used More than 6 months have passed since it became official. It can no longer be used as payment. Refer to management.

Voucher Masterlist

Under Settings you will find the full record of every voucher issued: Voucher #, Item, Value, Client, Issued (with its Valid Until date), Status, and Redemption. Status shows Expired automatically once a voucher's 6-month validity has passed, even though it was never redeemed or voided. It has Search and a Status filter (Active, Expired, Redeemed, Voided). This is where you look when someone asks about a voucher, and where you press 🖨 Download PDF to reprint one.

VIP Cards

A VIP Card is a product sold through Add VIP Card in Add Sale. Once added:

  • Every service in the transaction switches to VIP pricing — companions included.
  • The client is marked VIP in the Client Database, so VIP pricing applies on every future visit.
  • It has its own line in the Daily KPI and in the Sales Breakdown, separate from services and products.
Loyalty Cards are different

A Loyalty Card is not a VIP Card. The Loyalty Card Number sits on the client's record, and has its own monthly report — the Loyalty Card Sales Summary.

Chapter 10Payroll

Payroll takes attendance and commission and turns them into a payslip. Every staff member has an Individual Rate Setup as the basis.

Payroll now sits under the Admin Hub section of the sidebar (Chapter 26 onward) alongside Announcement, Memo, Staff Schedule, Leave Request, and Incident Report. Nothing about how Payroll itself works has changed — only where it appears in the menu.

Individual Rate Setup

Daily Rate (₱)
Pay for one full day of work.
Meal Allowance (₱)
Allowance once hours reach a full shift.
Overtime Meal Allowance (₱)
Additional allowance once hours pass the overtime threshold.
Secondary Role Daily Rate (₱)
For a Therapist who relieves as Receptionist. Used on days they chose Receptionist duty.
Bank Accounts
Bank Type, Specify Bank, Account Name, and Account Number — for releasing pay.

Payroll Group

The group decides both who appears and the scope used to compute their pay:

  • Branch group (e.g. Crown Head Spa Biñan) — staff on a Weekly compensation schedule assigned to that branch. Only attendance and commission recorded at that branch are counted.
  • Admin Staff — staff on a Bi-Monthly schedule. Attendance and commission are counted across all branches.
Working two branches in one day

If someone clocks in at two branches on the same date, that day is split by the share of hours spent at each. The two rows never add up to more than one day's pay. Commission, by contrast, stays whole and attributed to the branch where the sale happened.

How pay is computed

Therapist

Daily Rate paid in full for any day worked Meal Allowance at 9 hours or more (8 work + 1 break) OT Meal Allowance past 9 hours 15 minutes Commission from the services performed

Receptionist and Marketing Agent

Under 9 hours (Daily Rate ÷ 8) × (hours − 1 hour break) 9 hours or more full Daily Rate Overtime (Daily Rate ÷ 8) × 1.25 × (hours − 9) Meal Allowance none for these roles

Early arrival is excluded. Hours are counted from the official start of the shift — 9:00 AM for Opening, 1:00 PM for Closing.

The payslip

The payslip shows each day in the period: Date, Time In, Time Out, Daily Rate, Meal Allowance, Overtime, and Commission. Below that sit Additional Pay and Deduction where they apply. Status is either Pending or Paid.

There are three archives: Payroll Group Archive (closed payroll runs), Payslip Archive (per person), and My Payroll, where each staff member views their own payslip.

Before releasing payroll

Review Attendance for the whole period first. A missing or wrong clock-in becomes wrong pay directly. Once attendance is corrected the payslip updates by itself — there is nothing to recompute by hand.

Chapter 13Scheduling

This is where branch appointments are managed. It has four parts:

Branch Schedule
The selected day's timeline by bed and time.
Upcoming Schedules
Bookings still to come — Date, Client, Service, Branch, Schedule, Contact, Note, Status.
Blocked Dates
Days the branch is closed or not accepting bookings, with a Reason and Blocked By.
Schedule History
Past appointments, for looking back.

Creating an appointment

  1. Press Add Appointment.
  2. Enter the client, service, therapist, date, and time. Mobile Number and Email Address are optional — fill them in if you want a booking confirmation sent (auto-filled already if the appointment came from a web booking request).
  3. The system checks for a free bed and for therapist conflicts.
  4. Press Save Schedule.
  5. If a mobile number or email address was entered, a Send booking confirmation? popup appears with an Email and an SMS checkbox — both unchecked by default. Check whichever you want sent, then press Save Appointment to finish saving.
  6. It now appears on the Dashboard, and the therapist receives a notification.

Statuses are Scheduled, Confirmed, Completed, and Cancelled.

About the confirmation popup

A checkbox is only checkable if that contact detail was actually entered on the appointment — Email needs an email address, SMS needs a mobile number. The Email confirmation is a branded email with the booking details and any companions. SMS sends a short text via Semaphore — the Semaphore account needs both an approved Sender Name (Semaphore dashboard → Sender Names — takes a few hours to a day to get approved) and loaded SMS credits, or the SMS checkbox will fail with an alert showing the exact reason (the appointment itself still saves either way).

A faster route

When the client is in front of you and books their next visit, use Add to Schedule inside the Add Sale form. It finds a free bed and time automatically, so you never have to open Scheduling.

Taking a bed offline for the day

Each bed's column in the Branch Schedule timeline has an Available checkbox, checked by default. Uncheck it to take that bed out of service for the day currently shown:

  • No new appointment can be placed in that bed on that day — the column is greyed out and the bed shows as "(Unavailable)" if picked from the bed dropdown when creating or editing an appointment.
  • If the bed already has an appointment that day, you're asked to confirm first — the existing appointment is not removed or moved, unchecking only blocks new ones.
  • The public website's booking page counts one less bed for that branch on that day, so guests can't request a slot the bed can't actually serve.

Re-check the box to bring the bed back into service. This only ever applies to the specific day shown — it resets to available again the next day unless unchecked again.

Chapter 14Booking Requests

This is where bookings from the public Crown Head Spa website arrive. They are not appointments yet — they are requests waiting to be reviewed.

Each row shows Branch, Service, Preferred Date / Time, Client, Contact, Notes, Submitted, and Hold Status. Hold Status tells you whether a slot is being temporarily held for that request.

  1. Read the request and check it against the branch's schedule.
  2. Call or message the client using the contact details they supplied.
  3. Press Update to open the request and log what happened — see Logging remarks below.
  4. If they confirm, create the real appointment in Scheduling.
  5. If it cannot be accommodated, press Decline.
No automatic reply is sent

Declining does not notify the visitor. You still have to contact them yourself. Check this page daily — never let a request sit unanswered.

Logging remarks — Add Remark

Each pending row has one Update button. Pressing it opens the request in a window where you can log whether the client was reached before you act on the request.

  1. Type what happened in the Add Remark box — for example, whether the client answered, was texted, or could not be reached — and press Add Remark to save it.
  2. Add as many remarks as needed; each one is saved the moment you press the button and stays on the request for the next person who opens it.
  3. Press Update at the bottom of the window to close it and return to the Pending table, or go straight to Create Appointment / Decline from the same window.
Remarks cannot be edited or deleted

Once a remark is saved it is permanent — there is no edit or delete option, by design, so the record of who contacted the client and when cannot be altered afterwards. Each remark shows the nickname of the staff account that wrote it (or the account name, if that account has no nickname set) and the time it was added.

Looking back at handled requests

The main table only lists requests still waiting. Once you create an appointment from one, decline it, or it expires (see below), it leaves that list. To find it again, press Show History at the bottom of the page.

Previous Requests opens underneath with the same columns plus Outcome, which tells you whether an appointment was created, the request was declined, or it expired, when that happened, and (for created/declined) which staff account did it. Nothing here can be acted on again — it is for reference.

Expired requests

If a request is still sitting in Pending after its requested date has passed with no staff action, the system automatically marks it Expired and moves it into Previous Requests — the client's slot is gone by then either way. This happens on its own; no one needs to decline it manually. Check the page daily so requests don't reach this point unanswered.

Press View Remarks under a row's Outcome to read every remark that was logged on that request while it was still pending — the same read-only record described above.

Finding a particular day

History opens showing everything. To narrow it, use the Date submitted picker at the top right of that section, or step a day at a time with the ‹ and › buttons beside it. Show all clears the filter and brings the whole list back.

The date is when it arrived

The picker matches the day the request came in, not the day the guest asked to visit. Looking for "the one that came in on Tuesday" is what this is for; the guest's requested date is still there to read in the Preferred Date column. A request that arrives late at night counts under the day you received it.

Pending requests do not expire on their own

What expires is the temporary slot hold, which is why a request can sit in the pending list showing Expired under Hold Status. The request itself stays pending forever until someone acts on it, so the Pending count keeps growing. Decline the ones that are no longer live so the list stays a true picture of what still needs your attention — and they will still be findable under Show History afterwards.

Chapter 15Warehouse and Branches

Three pages make up inventory, and they are used in sequence.

Inventory Settings — the master list

The request flow

Branch requests → status: Awaiting Response → a badge appears on the Warehouse sidebar item Warehouse answers → Send Stock out to the branch Branch receives → it is added to the branch's Available Stock

Stock that deducts itself

Once an item is linked to a service in Inventory Settings, you no longer have to subtract it by hand. Saving a sale in the Daily Income Report does it for you:

Sale saved with a service → every item linked to that service becomes a Stock Audit row → carrying the sale's date, therapist and client → and the same quantity comes off the branch's Available Stock

Editing that sale afterwards corrects both. Add a service and its items are deducted; remove one and the stock goes back; leave a service alone and its row stays put, keeping its Serial No. and anything you typed into it by hand. Deleting or voiding the sale returns everything it took.

You can still edit a Stock Audit row yourself — the Serial No. especially, which the system cannot know. What you type is kept even when the sale is saved again.

Nothing happens until you link an item

Deduction only runs for items that have a service linked to them. Until then the Stock Audit table stays empty and stock figures behave exactly as they always have. Start with one item, save one real sale that uses that service, and check the audit row and the stock it took before you link the rest.

A branch cannot go below zero

If a branch has already run out, the sale still saves and the audit row still appears, but only what was actually there comes off. The row records what was really taken, so voiding that sale later gives back the true amount and never hands the branch stock it never had. A branch showing zero while treatments are still being sold means it needs restocking — request it from the warehouse.

Chapter 23System Health and Database

  • Admin only

The Data Protection Center is the most important page in the system. It reports the state of the Database, the Last Backup, Storage Used, and the Version.

Backup — every day

  1. Press Export Full Backup.
  2. A JSON file downloads containing all of CrownOS — users, branches, services, products, therapists, clients, schedules, income, expenses, and settings.
  3. Keep two copies: one on the computer, one on Google Drive, iCloud, or a USB drive.
When to do it

After the day's Daily Income Report is complete. If no backup has been taken today, a dot appears next to System Health / Database in the sidebar as a reminder.

Restore

  1. Press Choose Backup File and select the JSON file.
  2. CrownOS verifies it and shows the backup date, who created it, how many records it holds, and what it covers.
  3. Read the verification panel. Confirm this is the right backup.
  4. Press Restore Selected Backup and confirm.
Restoring replaces current data

A restore returns the whole system to how it stood when the backup was made. Everything done since that date is lost. Before restoring, export a fresh backup of the current state — so there is something to return to if you pick the wrong file.

Emergency Export

Produces CSV files you can open in Excel — for Clients, income records, and schedules. Use it when something has to be handed outside the system, or when there is an access problem.

How data syncs

Every change saves to the browser first and is pushed straight to Crown Head Spa's central database. All branches and devices see the same data.

  • If the internet drops — CrownOS keeps working. Changes queue up and are sent automatically when the connection returns.
  • If two people edit the same thing — the last one to save wins. So it is best not to have two people fixing the same day at once.
  • Always log out — logging out makes sure your last changes reach the cloud before you leave.

Chapter 32Troubleshooting

The page shows no data.

Most likely no Branch is selected, or the Date is wrong. Check the toolbar at the top first. If both look right, press Refresh.

"Your account does not have permission to access this page."

That page is not part of your role. Check the access matrix. If you genuinely need it for your work, an Admin can grant it through Additional Access in Account Settings.

I forgot to clock in or out.

Tell an Admin, Executive Assistant, or Receptionist. They will add the correct times in Attendance using Add Attendance Entry. The payslip updates by itself afterwards.

It says I am too far from the branch when clocking in.

You need to be within 150 metres of the branch's GPS pin. Turn on location on your phone and try again from inside the branch. With a weak signal the entry is marked "No GPS" — it still goes through, but an Admin should review it in Attendance.

A service came up at the wrong price.

Check the price type set on that line — Regular, First Timer, VIP, or Senior/PWD. If VIP appears unexpectedly, the client is probably already VIP in the database, or a VIP Card is in the transaction. If the price itself is genuinely wrong, an Admin or Executive Assistant corrects it in the List of Services.

The system will not accept a voucher code.

Read the exact message — Chapter 8 lists each one and what it means. Search the Voucher Masterlist by the client's name to see the code's real status.

I settled the wrong transaction.

If that row has an Edit option, fix it there. If not, an Admin or Executive Assistant can correct it. Do not use Clear This Branch / Date — that deletes the entire day, not the one mistake.

There is a "🧪 Local Test" badge at the top.

You are on a test copy, not the real CrownOS — nothing you type is being saved. Report it immediately and stop entering data until it reads ☁ Synced to Cloud again.

The internet dropped mid-shift.

Carry on working. Your changes queue up and send automatically when the connection returns. Before closing, make sure the internet is back and you can see ☁ Synced to Cloud before logging out.

I cannot sign in even though the password is right.

The account list may still be downloading from the cloud. Wait a few seconds and try again. If it persists, check whether your account is Inactive — only an Admin can reactivate it in Account Settings.

This device won't sync — or can't even log in — no matter what I try.

This can happen when the browser's storage on that specific device is full — it shows the same way whether you're on WiFi or mobile data, and whether you're already logged in elsewhere or not. Restarting the app does not fix it by itself, because the storage stays full. The fix is to clear that site's data on the affected device:

  • iPhone/iPad (Safari or Chrome): Settings → Safari (or Chrome) → Advanced → Website Data → find the CrownOS site → Delete, then reopen the app.
  • Android/desktop Chrome: open the site, then the lock/info icon beside the address bar → Site settings → Clear data, then reload.

This only needs to be done once per affected device — it re-downloads everything fresh afterward. Tell an Admin if it keeps recurring on the same device.
